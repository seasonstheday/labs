Games 1 and 3 use the grip model from the video: a spider's feet hook tiny bumps, and glazed enamel or clean glass has none. The needle in Game 3 is his grip; smoother surfaces knock it about harder and give him a narrower safe band. Game 2 is a flashlight search scored on the real survey, Bertone and others (2016): 50 houses in Raleigh, North Carolina, every one with spiders, about 77% of bedrooms and 68% of bathrooms. House spiders almost never bite people. The one in the tub just wants out.
