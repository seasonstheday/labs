# Why, Dad? Defender: inventory, upgrades and economy

A benchmark of how comparable mobile games handle items, tiers, upgrades and unlocks, set against what Defender does today (build 25), with a proposal for a **Pages** system to unlock friends, daily and weekly missions, and an item upgrade path. October 2026.

## The short version

1. **Legendary stops meaning anything.** Drop odds climb with the stage, so from stage 20 more than half of all drops are Legendary, and from stage 30 it is 82–85%. A late-game drop is better than anything the Store sells.
2. **Items have no future.** A drop can be worn, given bookmarks or sold. There is no way to make a favourite item better, and duplicates are only worth coins. Every comparable game gives duplicates a job.
3. **Friends join two different ways.** Each friend joins free after a set stage, or earlier from a random Library Card draw (30% a friend, a guaranteed one every 5 draws). The random path is the only real randomness in unlocking, and it's the part the industry and regulators are moving away from.
4. **There are no missions.** The only daily reason to return is the 7-day gift calendar.
5. **The coin economy is healthy.** First clears of all 40 stages pay about 18,400 coins. Maxing every rank, skill and weapon costs about 37,000. Players need replays and stars for the rest, which is the right shape.

The proposal: **Pages** replace the random friend draw with a visible, choose-your-own path; **missions** feed Pages and coins every day and week; **merging and polishing** give items a future and a coin sink; and the **drop curve** is reset so Legendary comes from merging and bosses, not from every chest late in the game.

## How comparable games do it

| Game | Items and tiers | Upgrading items | Unlocking characters | Daily pull |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Archero** | Common → Great → Rare → Epic → Perfect Epic → Legendary | Three identical items of one rarity fuse into the next. Perfect Epic and Legendary come **only** from fusion. The fused item keeps the highest level, and fusing refunds the coins and scrolls spent on the two used up | Gems, or hero shards from events (10 to 70 shards per star in the original) | Events, energy |
| **Survivor.io** | Grey → Green → Blue → Purple (+1, +2) → Yellow → Red | Three of the same rarity and slot merge up. "+" steps take any item of that rarity and slot; the next full tier needs the exact item. Levels cost coins plus slot-specific designs. Items can be downgraded to recover materials | Characters from shards and events | Daily tasks worth 10–20 points each; 100 points opens the chests. Weekly tasks the same over a week |
| **Clash Royale** | Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Champion cards | Duplicate cards plus gold level a card. Supercell has twice lowered mid-level costs to keep the pace even | Cards from chests, the shop and trophy road | Quests, the pass |
| **Brawl Stars** | Brawlers by rarity | Power points and coins | Removed all loot boxes in December 2022 for a visible reward track paid in Credits. In 2026 it's "Brawler Blast": Credits fill toward the next Brawler (Rare 160, Epic 925, Legendary 3,800). Supercell credits the change as part of the game's turnaround | Quests, pass, trophy road |
| **Hero shard games** (Mobile Legends, Dragon Champions and others) | — | — | Collect N fragments of a hero to unlock it. Rarer heroes need more. Extra fragments star the hero up. Fragments come from daily logins, events and quests | Login and quest fragments |
| **Prodigy Math** (kids, education) | Pets and gear | Pet evolutions | Many pets and evolutions behind a membership | Constant membership prompts, which parents, teachers and kids complain about |

### What the benchmark says

- **Duplicates always have a job.** Fuse them (Archero, Survivor.io), level with them (Clash Royale) or star up with them (shard games). Defender's only answer is "sell".
- **The top tier is made, not found.** Archero's Legendary and Survivor.io's top tiers come from merging. That keeps the top tier rare without making drops feel bad.
- **Unlocks are moving from chance to a visible track.** Brawl Stars dropped boxes for a bar that fills toward a known next unlock. Shard systems show the target and the count.
- **Daily missions are small, points-based and finishable.** Survivor.io's dailies are worth 10–20 points each, and 100 points opens the chest. A free player can always finish them.
- **Kids' games get judged on their paywalls.** Prodigy's reviews are a list of what parents resent: content locked behind a membership and constant prompts.

## The rules a kids' game has to live inside

- **Paid random rewards are the risk.** Apple and Google both require the odds to be shown before buying any random item. Belgium's regulator treats paid loot boxes as illegal gambling, and the Netherlands is pushing for an EU-wide ban. Defender is safe today only because gems can't be bought with money. **If gem packs are ever sold, the Library Card draw becomes a paid loot box.** Pages remove that problem for good.
- **Kids Category apps need a parental gate** before any purchase or link out of the app, and Apple says ads must be human-reviewed for age. The game already gates purchases and the video offer.
- **The FTC's 2022 Epic Games settlements ($520 million)** were about interface design that nudged purchases and about children's data. The lesson for us: no pre-checked purchase steps, no pressure timers, no locking content a child has already earned, and refunds as easy as buying.

## Proposal

### 1. Pages: the friend's storybook

Each friend has a storybook. Collect its pages to unlock the friend. Once unlocked, more pages raise their stars.

**Pages to unlock**

| Friend | Pages | Today they join free after |
|---|---|---|
| Webster | 5 | stage 1 |
| Hopper | 10 | stage 2 |
| Sparky | 10 | stage 3 |
| Puddles | 15 | stage 4 |
| Dusty | 25 | stage 13 |
| Pip | 25 | stage 18 |
| Scuttle | 30 | stage 23 |
| Nilla | 30 | stage 28 |
| Luna | 40 | stage 38 |

**Two kinds of page**
- **Friend pages** belong to one friend. Every first clear in a chapter pays pages of that chapter's friend, enough that the friend joins **no later than today's free stage**. Nobody is ever slower than now.
- **Blank pages** go to any friend the child chooses. They come from daily gifts, mission chests and finished Diary topics. Choosing is the point: a child who loves the owl can get the owl sooner.

**Stars after unlocking.** Extra pages raise a friend from ★1 to ★5.

| Star | Pages | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| ★2 | 10 | +4% Power and Quick |
| ★3 | 20 | +4% more, and a sticker for their Party card |
| ★4 | 30 | +4% more |
| ★5 | 40 | +4% more, and a gold frame |

That is 100 pages per friend past unlock, and +16% in all. Stars are a slow, long-term sink for duplicate pages, like Clash Royale levels and Archero stars, and small enough that they don't decide fights.

**The Library Card becomes "pick a page".** Instead of a hidden random draw, a card shows three face-up choices, and the child picks one. For example: 5 pages of a friend, 2 Blank pages, or one Rare+ item. Nothing is hidden before the choice, so it isn't a loot box under any store's or country's rules, now or if gems are ever sold. The pity counter goes away.

**The Big Book Pass** keeps its promise: every friend joins at once. Pages then only feed stars, so paying never removes a goal the child is chasing.

### 2. Missions

**Daily: three small missions.** Each is finishable in one or two matches. Each pays 1 star toward a chest, and 3 stars open the daily chest (coins, 1–2 Blank pages, sometimes a bookmark).
- Pop 60 myths.
- Use one friend's ability 15 times.
- Learn a new fact or answer the pop quiz right.
- Win a stage with a friend you haven't used today.
- Clear any stage with 3 stars.

**Weekly: five missions.** Each is worth one stamp, and 5 stamps open the weekly chest (a friend's pages of the child's choice, gems, an Epic item).
- Clear a chapter boss.
- Merge two items.
- Finish a Diary topic.
- Play on three different days.
- Bust 500 myths.

**Story missions: one-time.** Long goals that pay a friend's pages and gems: bust every Pond myth, clear a stage on Hard, reach ★3 with any friend, finish every Library topic.

**Kid-friendly rules.** No timers on screen, no penalty for a missed day, unfinished dailies just roll over to new ones, and nothing asks for money from the mission screen.

### 3. Items: merge, polish and a new drop curve

**Merge.** Three items of the **same slot and rarity** become one of the next rarity: Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary. The child picks which base survives (keeping their favourite name and look). Bookmarks come back to the bag. Any same-slot match counts, as in Survivor.io's "+" steps, so a child isn't hunting one exact item.

**Polish.** Spend coins to raise an item from +0 to +5. Each step adds 10% to all its stats. Cost per step goes up with rarity.

| Rarity | +1 | +2 | +3 | +4 | +5 | To +5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 20 | 40 | 60 | 80 | 100 | 300 |
| Rare | 40 | 80 | 120 | 160 | 200 | 600 |
| Epic | 60 | 120 | 180 | 240 | 300 | 900 |
| Legendary | 100 | 200 | 300 | 400 | 500 | 1,500 |

Merging refunds polish coins from the two items used up, as Archero does, so polishing early never feels wasted. A full set of 30 Legendary items at +5 is a 45,000-coin late-game sink, which matches the replay and Hard-mode income that currently has nowhere to go.

**The new drop curve.** Drops stop at Epic. Legendary comes from merging, chapter bosses (one guaranteed Epic-or-better per boss), story missions and the named one-offs.

| Stage | Common | Rare | Epic | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | 65% | 30% | 5% | merge only |
| 11–20 | 50% | 38% | 12% | merge only |
| 21–30 | 35% | 45% | 20% | merge only |
| 31–40 | 25% | 50% | 25% | merge only |

**Store offers follow.** The Lunchbox stays coins-only. The Backpack becomes "one Epic and two Rare", shown up front. Every Store box lists its contents and odds on the button.

### 4. Currencies

Keep 🪙 coins and 💎 gems. **Library Cards become a page currency only**: they never hold a hidden prize. Pages are not a currency to spend in the Store; they only come from play, gifts and missions. That keeps the money side of the game far from the friend side.

## Rollout

| Phase | What ships | Size |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The new drop curve, merging, polishing, Store boxes showing contents | small |
| 2 | Pages: storybooks on the Party screen, chapter page rewards, Blank pages, "pick a page" cards, stars | medium |
| 3 | Daily, weekly and story missions with their chests, and the daily calendar paying Blank pages | medium |
| 4 | A tuning pass with the simulator: time to unlock each friend, coins against sinks, a free player's week | small |

## Decisions for you

1. **Stars or no stars.** Stars give duplicate pages a purpose and a long tail, but they're one more number. The alternative is converting duplicate pages into coins.
2. **Can pages ever be bought?** I recommend never, so paying only ever buys the Pass and cosmetics. That's the cleanest line for a kids' game.
3. **What happens to old saves.** Owned friends stay owned. Leftover Library Cards turn into Blank pages at one card to three pages. Any item above the new curve stays as it is.
