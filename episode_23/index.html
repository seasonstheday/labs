The color is the size of the step. Slide the step, then drop the electron.
Game 1 is the series loop from the video: fifty bulbs share the wall's 120 volts, 2.4 each; a burnt bulb's shunt keeps the loop closed (so it is not the problem), while a loose bulb opens the loop and darkens all fifty. Leave burnt bulbs in and the rest run hotter: ten dark ones push the others to 3 volts, about a tenth of the life. Game 2: an LED's color is the size of the step the electron drops, 1240 divided by the step in electron-volts gives the wavelength in nanometers; a white LED is a blue one under a yellow coating. Numbers rounded.
