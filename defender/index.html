How to play

  1. Dad never moves. Grumpy Myths march in from every side. Once they step inside his attention ring, his gear attacks on its own.
  2. Popped myths drop purple "why?" orbs (level up, pick new gear) and 🪙 coins. Tap a gear slot at the bottom to spend coins on it mid-match.
  3. Every match earns 💡 Knowledge. Spend it on the title screen: the Garage makes gear permanently stronger, the Closet dresses Dad in things that help.
  4. Every myth that pops turns into a real fact, saved in your Fact Book.
  5. Myths that reach Dad use up his Patience. When it's gone, Dad needs a nap. Tap a myth to tell Dad to focus on it.
