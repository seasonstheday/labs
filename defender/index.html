How to play

  1. Nobody moves. Dad and his friends stand in the backyard while grumpy Myths march in. Once a myth steps inside Dad's attention ring, the party attacks on its own.
  2. Each character has one weapon and three abilities. Popped myths drop purple orbs; fill the bar to rank up an ability. Ranks last for the match.
  3. Gold orbs are 🪙 coins, and every match pays more at the end, plus a few random items. Equip them on the Party screen. 💎 Gems come from daily visits and first clears; 🎟️ Library Cards draw new friends.
  4. Every myth that pops turns into a real fact, saved in your Fact Book.
  5. Myths that reach Dad use up his Patience. When it's gone, Dad needs a nap. Tap a myth to focus everyone on it.
