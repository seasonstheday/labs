How to play

  1. Dad never moves. Grumpy Myths march in from every side. His gear attacks on its own.
  2. Popped myths drop purple "why?" orbs. Fill the bar to level up and pick new gear from the garage: yo-yos, paper airplanes, a baseball bat…
  3. Every myth that pops turns into a real fact. They're saved in your Fact Book.
  4. Myths that reach Dad use up his Patience. When it's gone, Dad needs a nap.
  5. Tap a myth to tell Dad to focus on it.
