How to play

🕷️Nobody moves. Myths walk in. Your team pops them on its own.
👆Tap a myth and everyone goes after it.
⭐Pick a card when you level up. Each one makes a trick stronger.
💗Myths that reach Dad cost Patience. Run out and Dad needs a nap.
📔Every popped myth is a real fact in your Diary. Facts you know make myths weaker.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑Friends join with pages, grow with levels, and wear things from your Bag.
