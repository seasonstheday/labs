How to play

  1. Nobody moves. Dad and his friends stand in the backyard while grumpy Myths march in. Once a myth steps inside Dad's attention ring, the party attacks on its own.
  2. Each character has one weapon and three abilities. Popped myths drop purple orbs; fill the bar to rank up an ability. Rank 5 of a weapon evolves it. You get two "Nope" cards a match if you don't like the choices.
  3. Tap a myth outside the ring and Dad shouts "Hey, you!" so it hurries over. Tap the lawn between waves to start the next one early.
  4. Gold orbs are 🪙 coins, and every match pays more at the end, plus a few random items. Equip them on the Party screen. 💎 Gems come from daily visits and first clears; 🎟️ Library Cards draw new friends.
  5. Every myth that pops turns into a real fact, saved in your Fact Book.
  6. Myths that reach Dad use up his Patience. When it's gone, Dad needs a nap. Tap a myth to focus everyone on it.
  7. Every stage has Easy, Normal and Hard and up to three stars: one for clearing, two if Dad kept more than half his Patience, three if nothing ever reached him.
  8. The Reading Room opens at party level 10: one boss, three questions from your own Fact Book, a bookmark if you win. Three visits per twelve hours.
  9. Everything in this game stays on your device. No accounts, no ads, nothing to buy with real money.
