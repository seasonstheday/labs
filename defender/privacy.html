Privacy: Why, Dad? Defender

Last updated: October 2026.

Why, Dad? Defender is a game for curious children and their grown-ups. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing of our own. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no server of ours. Your game is saved on your device.

Game Center and iCloud

If you are signed in to Game Center, a copy of your saved game is stored in your own iCloud by Apple, so it can come back on a new device. We cannot see it. Apple's privacy policy covers it.

Video offers (Google AdMob)

The game can offer an optional short video in return for an in-game reward. These offers sit behind a grown-up check. Ads are requested as child-directed, non-personalised and rated G. We do not ask for permission to track you. To show and measure an ad, Google's software may process limited information such as an approximate location from your IP address, a device identifier for this app, and diagnostic data. See Google's privacy policy. Grown-ups who buy The Big Book Pass will not see video offers at all.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple (or Google Play) behind a grown-up check. We do not receive or keep payment details.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not ask for names, ages, photos, contacts or location.
