## Description (4000 max)

> Does a goldfish really forget everything in three seconds? Is the Moon made of cheese? Do bulls hate the colour red?
>
> Sir Ever-So-Sure, the pompous Title Page of the Big Book of Wrong, has flung the book open, and every silly idea anyone ever believed is marching into the backyard. Only Dad, the Kid and a team of backyard friends can stop them.
>
> **POP THE MYTHS**
> Myths walk in; your team pops them. Dad zaps them with sunlight from his magnifying glass, Hopper the toad licks a whole line at once, Sparky the firefly chains sparks from myth to myth. Pick a new trick every time the team levels up, and build a different team every time you play.
>
> **LEARN A REAL FACT EVERY TIME**
> Every myth you pop turns back into a true fact, and Dad reads it out loud. 145 myths about animals, space, the body, weather, food, history and more, each checked against a trusted source such as a museum, a university or NASA. Every fact goes into Dad's Diary, and every topic you fill makes the team stronger.
>
> **28 BACKYARD FRIENDS**
> Spiders, frogs, fireflies, ducks, owls, a goldfish in a bubble, a unicorn and many more, each with three moves to grow. Mix families for team bonuses and set off reactions such as Fizz, when water meets a spark.
>
> **AN 8-CHAPTER STORY ABOUT ASKING WHY**
> Chase Sir Ever-So-Sure from the backyard to the pond, the library, the ocean, the desert, the Arctic, the jungle and finally the night sky, with a hand-painted storybook scene at every turn and an original orchestral soundtrack.
>
> **MADE FOR CURIOUS KIDS AND THEIR GROWN-UPS**
> • Every level is free to play, any time. No energy timers.
> • Little Kid mode: slower myths, and Dad never runs out of Patience.
> • Read-aloud facts and buttons, bigger text, calm screen and colour-blind friendly symbols.
> • Grown-ups only: any video offer or purchase sits behind a grown-up check.
> • Progress saves to your phone and to iCloud through Game Center.
>
> No dodging. Only wondering.

(about 1,900 characters)
